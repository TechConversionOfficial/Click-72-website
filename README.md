## About the business

- **What it does:** Click 72 provides local SEO, Google Business Profile optimisation, website design and development, social media marketing, content marketing and paid campaigns, including Google and Meta advertising.
- **Who it serves:** Local businesses, service providers, retailers, startups and growing companies wanting to improve online visibility and receive more enquiries. The website names businesses such as salons, coaching institutes, gyms, real estate agents, cafés and interior designers.
- **Where it operates:** Its website identifies a Kolkata head office at 85 Park Street, with additional contact locations listed on its Contact page.
- **Why a clear website matters:** A visitor may know they want more enquiries without knowing whether their immediate problem is search visibility, advertising, content or their website. The website needs to make those choices understandable before asking someone to enquire.

## The website challenge

Digital marketing agencies offer services that overlap. A small-business owner may search for Google Maps visibility, ask about paid advertising and still need a website that converts interest into an enquiry. Presenting all these services without a clear structure risks confusing visitors or forcing them to make contact before understanding the offer.

The published Click 72 website brings its services together with a business-results message, industry examples, a short process and contact forms. **This describes the challenge the finished website is positioned to address; it is not a claim that a previous website was defective or rebuilt.**

The key communication priorities are:

1. Help a visitor recognise the service that fits their business need.
2. Explain what working with Click 72 could involve.
3. Provide an obvious route to a consultation or enquiry.
4. Present the agency consistently across desktop and mobile layouts.

## How the website presents the solution

1. **Begin with the customer's business goal.** The homepage opens with a website-and-leads proposition aimed at local businesses, rather than immediately listing technical marketing terms.
2. **Make service categories recognisable.** Google Business Profile optimisation, SEO and local SEO, paid campaigns, website development, social media marketing and content marketing appear as separate offerings.
3. **Show which businesses can benefit.** Industry examples include real estate agents, coaching institutes, fitness businesses, salons, retailers and local trades.
4. **Explain the engagement sequence.** Click 72 describes a strategy call, proposal and approval, execution and growth reporting, giving prospective clients an outline of what happens after an enquiry.
5. **Provide a direct next step.** Consultation and audit calls to action, together with contact forms, make the intended lead-generation purpose explicit.
6. **Use dedicated pages for further evaluation.** Visitors can read more about the agency, paid advertising and its contact locations without relying on a single homepage.
7. **Support enquiries on different devices.** The site can be approached from mobile or desktop browsers, although no independent responsive-device test was supplied for this README.

These observations describe the public website and its portfolio presentation; they are **not** a verified internal record of the design team's implementation process.

## Standards relevant to the website

The following are **evaluation references**, not certifications or claims that this specific project has passed a formal audit:

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): Review loading performance, interaction responsiveness and visual stability, particularly on mobile.
- [WCAG 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): Assess contrast, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, accessible form labels and meaningful image alternatives.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): Evaluate discoverability, crawl access and useful service-page content.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): Check that any organisation, local-business, breadcrumb and service markup is accurate and matches the visible content.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): Consider how enquiry forms collect and handle contact details and what disclosures or permissions apply.

No technical audit, performance report, accessibility certification or compliance assessment was supplied for this repository record.
